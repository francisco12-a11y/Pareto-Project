# Inverse Interview — 20 questions answered by Kasim's Second Brain

The method: instead of interviewing the founder, ask the 20 questions a strategist would ask, then answer them *only* from the vault. Every answer cites its source note. Where the vault is silent, it says so.

**1. Who exactly is this for — and who is it not for?**
For founders with traction ($1M+ or the equivalent) who are still the operator: every decision, every approval, every fire. Not for idea-stage wantrepreneurs — the method presupposes a business worth handing off. Kasim's own rooms select the same way: Driven Mastermind (agency owners), the Daily Sigh ("founders with traction"), his Oct 2026 event ("founders/entrepreneurs only"). *(identity.md, linkedin-posts-2026-09.md)*

**2. What is the one dream outcome, in the founder's own words?**
"I want my business to run without me." Kasim's version, verbatim: "You know you own a business when you can leave for 90 days and when you come back things are more efficient and more profitable." *(evolution-engine-framework.md)*

**3. Why do they believe they can't have it?**
The Control Paradox: they think stepping back hurts growth, so they overwork and refuse to let go — which is exactly what stunts growth. Plus identity: "if I do it, it does not count" runs their internal accounting. *(jargon.md, you-are-the-bottleneck)*

**4. What have they already tried that failed?**
Hiring "rockstars" who needed managing, SOPs nobody followed, VA agencies that took tasks off a list but not off their mind, "passive income" schemes. Kasim kills the whole category: "I don't believe in passive income any longer… anything that can yield passive value in the post-AI world is something AI can do." *(deep-wealth-ep466)*

**5. What is the actual mechanism — what do they do in the program?**
Delegate *projects, not tasks*, using Miracle Briefs (outcome · first principles · mandate · deadline · belief signal). Delegate messy. Set guardrails (financial/time/quality caps), never step-by-step checklists. Expect the 2-6-2: of ten tasks, two come back worse, six fine, two miracles — "make the top two the whole job." Then close the loop: "What information was missing from my initial brief that would have saved you time?" *(jargon.md, expect-miracles, delegate-messy)*

**6. Why 10 weeks?**
Time-to-first-result has to be short (value equation: time delay ↓). First delegation ships week one; ten weeks is enough for three full delegate-review-compound cycles and one installed weekly cadence. The timeframe itself comes from the vault's own offer math: "a Kasim 10-week coaching cohort — 20h." *(day5-notes.md)*

**7. Who is Kasim to make this promise — the credential stack?**
Six multimillion-dollar businesses, three exits. Built the #1-ranked Google Ads agency in the world — ~100 people, $100M+ ad spend under management, 40% margins against an industry benchmark of 20% — while never learning to run Google Ads ("I don't know how to run Google ads. I never have. I never will."). Coaches at $2,000/hr (listed). Amazon-bestselling author (Seven Critical Principles); co-author of HIRE. *(identity.md, deep-wealth-ep466, hire.md)*

**8. Why should a founder believe the method works for someone who isn't Kasim?**
Because it has already worked on other people: his third EA (hired at $1,200/mo as a Ukrainian student) ran his SoftBank-backed exit from a war zone and became his 50/50 partner in Pareto Talent; Pareto EA Agustina turned one "just decide" into a septic company doing ~$14M/yr with $1.5M in government contracts — Kasim has never worked a day in it; a 2.5-minute voice note to Ivan became the $490k self-storage buy (~$750k retail). The system manufactures operators. That's the point. *(ivan-bunin.md, septic-co MOC, delegate-messy)*

**9. What does the founder fear most right now, beyond the calendar?**
That AI ends their advantage on a timetable they can't see. Kasim's own series says it: "AI as mass extinction — kills ~75%+ of businesses fast; survivors are the 25%," and "the race isn't against AI — it's against the founder in your space who figured this out months before you did." *(linkedin-posts-2026-09.md, strategy-that-built-your-business)*

**10. How does the cohort answer that fear without becoming an AI course?**
It's an AI-*leadership* cohort disguised as a delegation cohort: the way you survive rule changes is a business that adapts without you being the adapter. "AI isn't coming for your job. It's coming for your rigidity." The Evolution Engine — awareness → experiment → delegate → automate → cull — runs on other people's time, not on prompting tricks. *(evolution-engine-framework.md)*

**11. What do they get, concretely, line by line?**
Twenty hours live with Kasim across 10 weeks; their Second Brain built with them; 12 months in the founder community; the HIRE playbook (the Pareto Talent Framework — 7 steps, flytraps, paid trials); the Seven Laws of Delegation series. Stack: $46,400. Price: $3,000. *(day5-notes.md for the core three components and price)*

**12. Why is it $3,000 and not $30,000 — or $300?**
$3,000 is the vault's own number ("≈ $30k stack, sell at $3k"). It prices against his $2,000/hr rate — ten weeks costs less than 90 minutes of his consulting — while staying inside "scary but signable" for a founder who's never bought coaching. Anti-anchor: $300 would select for people who want the outcome without the letting-go. *(day5-notes.md, hormozi-method.md)*

**13. What's the guarantee, and why that shape?**
Conditional: finish the weeks, ship the ten Miracle Briefs, and if the business doesn't run measurably better without you, show the briefs and get 100% back. Conditional on purpose — the method's only failure mode is a founder who won't let go, and the guarantee self-selects those out. Brand lineage: Pareto ships a lifetime replacement guarantee on every EA. *(hormozi-method.md guarantee types, pareto-talent MOC)*

**14. What makes this scarce and urgent for real?**
One room at a time — Kasim runs a portfolio; the cohort is the one thing he teaches live. Small by design (first Pareto cohort: 11 people). Hard start date, applications close when seats fill. "A live event outsells a course because of true urgency." *(day5-notes.md, hormozi-method.md)*

**15. What does the founder lose by waiting a cohort cycle?**
Six months of their life at current load, and — the sharper cost — the latency tax: information decays between having the idea and delegating it polished. Every week undelegated is context evaporating. *(jargon.md)*

**16. What objection comes from people who 'already tried delegating'?**
"I delegated and it came back worse." Answer in Kasim's own arithmetic: that's two of ten — the 2-6-2 standard. Expect it, plan for it, and make the two miracles the entire job. Also: they delegated *tasks*, not projects — "messy delegation creates architects. Clean delegation creates robots." *(expect-miracles, delegate-messy)*

**17. What objection comes from people who 'can't afford the time'?**
They are the time. "You want to be the dumbest, least capable human that works for you" — and the cohort's first handoff ships in week one, so the calendar breaks even inside the program, not after it. *(expect-miracles)*

**18. What is Kasim's story in one paragraph — the trust accelerator?**
Raised by a blind single mother on Social Security in Albuquerque; sold candy at school (10¢ → 25¢); college dropout; a failure list that "reads like a cemetery" (medical transcription, software, furniture, mercury purification for amateur gold miners); then six multimillion-dollar businesses and three exits, including the #1-ranked Google Ads agency in the world. Autism diagnosed in his late thirties: "failure just feels like data." He hit $12M net worth, noticed "is this all there is?", and now teaches the machine that got him there. *(identity.md, deep-wealth-ep466, kasim-voice-notes.md)*

**19. Why does the page lead with 'You are the bottleneck' instead of the rags-to-riches story?**
His voice doctrine: contrarian claim first, story second, framework named last. The reader's status quo gets named before the founder's bio gets told — the bio is proof, not the hook. The story shows up where it converts: About. *(kasim-voice-notes.md)*

**20. What does success look like 90 days after the cohort?**
The founder's calendar has holes in it. Ten jobs live with other humans, two of them running better than the founder ever ran them. A weekly mutation meeting that happens whether or not the founder attends. And the test: they leave for a stretch, and the business gets *more* efficient while they're gone. *(evolution-engine-framework.md, you-are-the-bottleneck)*
