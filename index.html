02 · The problem

Your business grew. Onto you.

  • You approve everything, so nothing moves without you.
  • You hire help, then spend your evenings managing the help.
  • Vacations come with a laptop.
  • Your best ideas die in the gap between having them and handing them over. You know it.

The delegation books told you to let go. Nobody told you what to hand over, or to whom, or what comes back. So you stayed in the chair.

Meanwhile, a clock you can't see. AI compresses the life of everything that works: copywriting went from experiment to commodity in 18 months, and a $400 resume service died six months after ChatGPT shipped. The strategy that built your business is now killing it.

The race isn't against AI. It's against the founder in your space who figured this out months before you did.
03 · The cohort

Ten weeks, plainly.

WEEKS 1–3

Get it out of your head.

You build your Second Brain, the capture system for everything only you know. Your first Miracle Brief ships in week one: outcome, first principles, mandate, deadline, belief signal. Export chaos, import order. That's the deal.

WEEKS 4–7

Hand off the ten.

Delegate messy. Guardrails, never checklists. Of ten delegations, two come back worse, six fine, two better than you could do. That's the 2-6-2, and the two are the whole job. You'll hunt miracles, not mistakes.

WEEKS 8–10

Install the engine.

The weekly mutation meeting. The optionality map. Core and Edge: one part of the business exploits, one experiments. From here, the machine starts deciding without you.

You leave with ten jobs off your plate, a capture system that survives you, and a cadence. Not a binder.
